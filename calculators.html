Which one to open first

Start with the board calculator if you are buying decking. The count depends on which way the boards run, the real face width, and the gap. A 12×16 is not one number.

Open stain after the deck is built, or when you are pricing a refinish of a deck you already have. Coverage is area times coats, divided by the square feet per gallon on the label.

Open footings only for round cardboard tubes. It does not size a footing, pick a depth below frost, or design a bell. It converts a cylinder into bags.

Open balusters when the posts are already spaced, or when you want a shopping count for a straight run or a closed pen. A 4 inch opening is a common guard rule of thumb. Your inspector can require something else.

The guides walk through the same math with worked sizes.
